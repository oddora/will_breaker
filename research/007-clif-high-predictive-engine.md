## The Web Bot: Core Methodology

### What It Does

Web Bot is a system that **scrapes massive amounts of publicly accessible internet text** (forums, blogs, news, social media) and uses **predictive linguistics** to detect subtle patterns indicating upcoming societal, economic, and physical events.

### The Method — Step by Step

Clif High's own description (from Language Magazine interview):

> *"My process works by way of the **delta of language** expressed in common contexts over time **reduced to archetypes** and projected forward based on **numeric quantifiers for emotional values** assigned to words."*

#### 1. Corpus Collection ("Spyders")
- Programs (colloquially called "spyders" by High) continuously scrape the internet
- Multibyte character encoding determines language at point of encounter
- API-based automated translation reduces all samples to a core set of languages (English, Cyrillics, Greek, Sanskrit variants, Indonesian, etc.)
- Over 40 languages sampled; Chinese blocked by government for "superstition"

#### 2. Emotional Quantifier Lexicon
- High created a custom lexicon linking words to **arrays of emotional values**
- Each word gets numeric tags for: **time, intensity, duration of impact, and other emotional quantifiers**
- The lexicon functions as a bridge between words and an emotional value array
- "High emotive value" words are what he calls **"immediacy values"** — words that produce immediate, emotive reactions

#### 3. Delta Detection (The Key Insight)
- **Not word counts.** High explicitly says: *"It is not the words being used today that are the real key to my forecasting; rather it is the delta as to why this particular word (outside of typical lexical framework for that conversation) that is of importance."*
- The system tracks when a word appears **outside its normal contextual range** — a single anomalous instance can trigger a forecast
- Example: The **"blondes on boats"** incident off Italy was predicted weeks ahead based on a single occurrence of the word "blondes" that was totally outside the normal range for the data set in which it appeared
- Word counts are used internally only to track "changing states of words as regards to their existent emotive tagging" — the ebb and flow from slang to "old age"

#### 4. Archetype Reduction & Projection
- Language data is reduced to **archetypes** (taxonomic, reflecting historical precedent)
- These archetypes are projected forward based on the emotional quantifiers
- The system produces "emotional traces" within collective awareness that precede manifestation

#### 5. Theoretical Foundation
- High claims **all humans are psychic** — we are "standing waves" that function as "antennae" receiving psychic energy
- Psychic impressions arrive with a **"template layer"** of information prior to manifestation
- This information "leaks out" through word choices outside the typical 11,000-word annual vocabulary of industrialized humans
- Events beyond our control are "not outside our perception" — they leak into language before manifesting physically

## ALTA: Asymmetric Linguistic Trend Analysis

This is the formal name for High's methodology. Key characteristics:

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Core technique | NLP + emotional quantifier lexicon + delta detection |
| Languages | 40+ (reduced to ~5 via translation) |
| Programs | 300+ operating executables |
| Languages used | C, C++, Prolog (AI language that self-compiles), Lisp, Perl, assembly |
| Validation | "Spyders" matching corpora in MSM against forecasts; reader-maintained accuracy spreadsheets |
| Focus | Low-frequency word anomalies, not high-frequency counts |

High describes his work as **"a specifically focused subset of corpus linguistics"** using "advanced automata of my own design crafted (primarily) in Prolog, an artificial intelligence language that is able to re-compile (thus alter its function based on new learning) while in operation."
