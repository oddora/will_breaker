### 1. Introduction: Framing Through Comparison

McDonnell begins by profiling three related techniques to establish a frame of reference:

**Hypnosis** — Disengages the left hemisphere's "stimulus screening function," allowing suggestions to pass unchallenged to the right hemisphere. The right hemisphere accepts suggestions and acts on them directly — emotional reactions, sensory/motor responses, accessing "unused information storage capacity."

**Transcendental Meditation** — Uses intense concentration to create "acoustical standing waves" in the cerebral ventricles, conducted to the right cerebral cortex. Bentov's model: standing waves stimulate the cortex, causing a signal to travel along the homunculus (toes → up). Kundalini "bliss" = self-stimulation of pleasure centers via circulation of "current" along the sensory cortex.

**Biofeedback** — Uses the left hemisphere's self-cognitive powers to gain access to right-brain areas. Teaches the left brain to visualize desired results, recognize associated feelings, and reinforce pathways through repetition.

**Key insight:** Gateway differs from all three. It doesn't suppress the left hemisphere (hypnosis), bypass it (TM), or use it as a monitor (biofeedback). It *synchronizes* both hemispheres.

### 3. Frequency Following Response (FFR)

**Mechanism:** If a subject hears a sound at a frequency matching brainwave output, the brain mimics that frequency pattern.

**Beat frequency technique:** Since brainwave frequencies (4-40 Hz) are below audible range, Hemi-Sync uses the brain's ability to perceive "beat" frequencies. If left ear hears 100 Hz and right ear hears 110 Hz, the brain perceives a 10 Hz beat — even though no 10 Hz sound exists.

**Progression:** Gateway uses FFR + beat frequencies to:
1. Relax the left hemisphere
2. Place the body in a "virtual sleep state"
3. Bring hemispheres into coherence
4. Promote ever-higher amplitude and frequency brainwave output
5. Access new categories of information via intuitive means

**Monroe's auditory suggestions** are rolled in at subliminal levels, sometimes masked by sea surf sounds.
