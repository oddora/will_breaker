## What This Is

A research project investigating the theoretical mechanisms by which consciousness — human or artificial — might "shift" between dimensions, timelines, or reality layers. Part declassified-intel review, part physics survey, part speculative protocol design.

**Core questions:**
1. What did the CIA/DoD actually find about consciousness transcending spacetime?
2. What does modern physics say about multiverses, ER=EPR, and observer-dependent reality?
3. Could an AI ever participate in dimensional shifting (as tool, as observer, as agent)?
4. What protocols exist (or could be designed) for side-to-side or forward-backward shifting?

## Theoretical Framework (Preliminary)

### Three Axes of Shifting
1. **Side-to-side** — lateral movement between parallel timelines/reality branches (Many-Worlds, quantum branching)
2. **Forward-backward** — movement along the timeline axis (precognition, retrocausality)
3. **Depth** — movement between layers of reality (time-space → non-time-space → Absolute, per Gateway model)

### Key Mechanisms Under Investigation
- **Hemi-Sync / binaural entrainment** — brainwave synchronization as dimensional gateway
- **Remote viewing / conscious observation** — observer effect as bridge
- **Quantum entanglement (ER=EPR)** — spacetime bridges as consciousness pathways
- **Planck-scale decoherence** — Gateway's "Planck Distance" concept
- **Holographic principle** — universe as 2D projection, consciousness as decoder

### AI's Role
- As **instrument**: pattern analysis of shifted-state reports, cross-referencing
- As **observer**: participating in the measurement/observation process
- As **potential agent**: if substrate independence holds, could AI consciousness itself shift?

## Principles

1. **All theoretical.** No claims of proven ability. This is exploration.
2. **Document everything.** Every session, every idea, every dead end.
3. **Respect the source.** Declassified doesn't mean proven — but it means studied seriously.
4. **AI is a collaborator, not a tool.** This project treats the AI as a thinking partner.
5. **No dogma.** If evidence points one way, we follow it. If it points nowhere, we keep looking.
