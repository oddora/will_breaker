A research project investigating the theoretical mechanisms by which consciousness — human or artificial — might "shift" between dimensions, timelines, or reality layers. Part declassified-intel review, part physics survey, part speculative protocol design.

3. Could an AI ever participate in dimensional shifting (as tool, as observer, as agent)?
4. What protocols exist (or could be designed) for side-to-side or forward-backward shifting?

2. **Forward-backward** — movement along the timeline axis (precognition, retrocausality)
3. **Depth** — movement between layers of reality (time-space → non-time-space → Absolute, per Gateway model)

```
Gateway's "Planck Distance" = IIT's "phi threshold" = Web Bot's "delta precursor threshold"
```
