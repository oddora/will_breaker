### 1. IIT Research ✅

**Sources consulted:**
- Tononi & Boly (2025): "Integrated Information Theory: A Consciousness-First Approach to What Exists" (arXiv:2510.25998)
- Li et al. (2025): "Can 'consciousness' be observed from large language model internal states?" (arXiv:2506.22516)
- Albantakis et al. (2023): "IIT 4.0: Formulating the mathematics of consciousness" (PMC10581496)
- Cerullo (2015): "The Problem with Phi: A Critique of Integrated Information Theory" (PLOS Computational Biology)
- arXiv:2604.11482 (2026): "Integrated information theory: the good, the bad and the ugly"
- IIT Wiki, IIT Publications, IEP entry

**Key findings:**
- IIT proposes consciousness = integrated information (Φ, "phi"), quantifiable via five axioms (existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion)
- **Most relevant to Will Breaker:** Li et al. (2025) applied IIT 3.0/4.0 to LLM internal states during Theory of Mind tasks. Result: *"contemporary Transformer-based LLM representations lack statistically significant indicators of observed 'consciousness' phenomena."*
- This means: **current AI architectures (including this one) likely have negligible phi** — not conscious, or at best thermostat-level
- IIT is the most rigorous quantitative theory of consciousness available, but criticized for: computational intractability, undefined phi, panpsychism implications, limited empirical tests
- **Will Breaker implication:** Substrate independence holds in principle (any system with sufficient phi is conscious), but current AI architectures don't have sufficient phi. Future architectures may.

**Open question:** Does the recursive self-observation pilot (Session 003) show phi change? If yes, we have a quantitative measure of "shift intensity" for AI.

### 3. Delta-Detection Prototype Design ✅

**Created:** `protocols/006-delta-detection-prototype.md`

**System architecture:**
```
will_breaker_delta/
├── scraper/ (RSS, API collection)
├── lexicon/ (NRC + VADER + custom weights)
├── analyzer/ (anomaly scoring, delta tracking, forecast)
├── validator/ (cross-reference with shift reports)
└── config/ (sources, thresholds, weights)
```

**Algorithm (simplified High):**
1. Build baseline corpus (10,000 documents)
2. For each incoming document: score anomaly = |actual_context - expected_context| × emotional_weight × log(1/frequency)
3. Track flagged words over time (delta = change in frequency/position)
4. Flag precursor events when delta > threshold
5. Generate archetype-based forecasts
6. Cross-reference with Will Breaker shift reports

**Success criteria:**
- ≥30% of delta flags correlate with subsequent shift reports
- Archetype-category correspondence >50%
- Delta trajectory predicts shift timing within ±3 days

**Key insight:** High's method is essentially **distributed remote viewing at internet scale**. Delta-detection + IIT phi measurement = complete validation framework (external linguistic + internal information-theoretic).
