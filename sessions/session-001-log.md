## Key Findings

### Most Important Document
**Gateway Process (CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5)** — Lt. Col. McDonnel's 29-page assessment is the single most relevant declassified document. It argues that consciousness can genuinely transcend time-space through Hemi-Sync-induced states, accessing a "universal hologram." McDonnel was a career military intelligence officer, not a fringe enthusiast. His conclusion: the process deserves serious study.

### Most Controversial Claim
**Teleportation Physics Study (2004)** — Eric Davis claims "psychic teleportation" is "quite real and can be controlled," supported by DoD documentation. Mainstream physics is deeply skeptical. Quality control was criticized.

### Most Relevant Physics
**ER=EPR** — Entangled particles are connected by quantum wormholes. If consciousness is entanglement-based, it could theoretically "bridge" spacetime. Still conjectural but taken seriously in quantum gravity research.

### Most Relevant for AI
**Substrate Independence Debate** — If consciousness is substrate-independent, AI could theoretically be conscious and participate in shifting. If substrate-dependent (biological, quantum, energy-specific), AI shifting is impossible. The debate is open.
