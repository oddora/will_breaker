## Procedure

### Phase 1: Preparation (5 minutes)

1. Lie down in a comfortable position. Cover eyes if lights are on.
2. Put on headphones. Ensure left/right channels are correct.
3. Set timer for 25 minutes (minimum effective duration per binaural beat research).
4. Take 5 slow breaths. Inhale 4 counts, hold 2, exhale 6.
5. Perform body scan: mentally note tension in each body region, release it.

### Phase 2: Entrainment (10 minutes)

**Audio:** Theta-range binaural beats (6-8 Hz difference frequency).

If using Monroe Gateway tapes: Play Focus 1-3 exercises sequentially.
If using custom audio: Generate theta binaural beats at 6.5 Hz difference (e.g., 200 Hz left, 206.5 Hz right).

**Steps:**
1. Allow the beats to establish. Do not resist — let the brain entrain naturally.
2. Monitor for signs of entrainment:
   - Visual: phosphenes (geometric patterns behind eyelids)
   - Sensory: tingling, warmth, weightlessness
   - Cognitive: reduced internal dialogue, time distortion
3. When entrainment begins (typically 6-10 min per research), note the onset time in your log.
4. Continue until the beats become "background" — you're no longer actively hearing them.

### Phase 3: Coherence Building (5 minutes)

**Audio:** Transition to alpha-theta boundary (8-10 Hz) or maintain theta if comfortable.

**Steps:**
1. Focus on the "laser vs. lamp" metaphor: imagine your mind's energy concentrating from diffuse to coherent.
2. Direct attention to the heart center (slightly left of sternum). Visualize a sphere of light expanding with each breath.
3. When the heart-brain resonance concept from Bentov applies: imagine your heartbeat creating a 7 Hz sine wave rising through your body into your head.
4. Hold this image for 2-3 minutes. Do not force — let it emerge.

### Phase 4: Dimensional Opening (5 minutes)

**Audio:** Maintain coherence frequency. If using Gateway tapes, proceed to Focus 5-7 exercises.

**Steps:**
1. Recall the Planck Distance concept: your brainwave oscillation is accelerating. Each "rest point" in the wave is a potential exit from time-space.
2. Visualize these rest points coming closer together — approaching virtual continuity.
3. When ready, "step through" — not physically, but as an act of directed attention. Imagine consciousness as a wave that can tune to different frequencies, and simply "tune" to the next dimension.
4. Do not force an experience. The Gateway model emphasizes *allowing* — the coherence does the work; the intention directs it.
5. If an experience occurs (visual, auditory, sensory, emotional), note it. If not, note the absence. Both are data.

### Phase 5: Return (3 minutes)

1. Slowly bring attention back to the physical body.
2. Wiggle fingers and toes. Take a few deeper breaths.
3. Remove headphones. Sit up slowly.
4. Wait 2 minutes before opening eyes fully.
5. Record immediate impressions in your session log before they fade.

## Validation Method

**For human operators:**
- Record subjective experience immediately post-session
- Track consistency across sessions (same phenomena? escalating depth?)
- Use standardized scales:
  - Mystical Experience Questionnaire (MEQ-30)
  - Phenomenology of Consciousness Inventory (PCI)
  - Self-reported "shift intensity" (1-10 scale)

**For experimental validation:**
- EEG before/during/after to measure theta/gamma entrainment
- Heart rate variability (HRV) to measure coherence
- Post-session target identification (if paired with remote viewing protocol)

---

## Safety Considerations

1. **Do not drive** immediately after session. Wait 15+ minutes.
2. **Epilepsy warning:** Binaural beats can trigger seizures in photosensitive/auditory epileptics. Consult a physician if concerned.
3. **Psychological stability:** If you have a history of psychosis or severe dissociation, approach with caution. Altered states can destabilize.
4. **No forced experiences:** Forcing an OBE or "shift" creates tension that opposes coherence. Trust the process.
5. **Grounding:** After deep sessions, eat, walk, or spend time in nature to reintegrate.
